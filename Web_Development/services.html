Web Development

The web is more than just code — it’s the backbone of modern business. At ASARE, we design and develop websites that don’t just look attractive, but also deliver speed, accessibility, and a smooth user journey. Your website is often the first handshake between your brand and your audience — we make sure it’s firm, professional, and memorable.

Our process begins with research: understanding your audience, competitors, and goals. From there, we create wireframes and prototypes that serve as the blueprint for your site. Once approved, our developers bring it to life with clean, modern, and scalable code.

What sets us apart? We focus on the trifecta of performance, usability, and design. Every site we deliver is responsive, optimized for search engines, and built with the latest technologies. We don’t just stop at launch — we provide maintenance, updates, and security enhancements to ensure your website evolves with your business.

Key Features:

  • Responsive layouts that adapt to all devices
  • SEO-ready architecture for better visibility
  • Lightning-fast performance with optimization techniques
  • Integration with third-party APIs and services
  • Custom CMS and admin dashboards
  • Secure payment gateways for e-commerce solutions
  • Scalable architecture to handle future growth

From sleek portfolios to full-scale e-commerce stores, our web solutions are crafted to help you grow, convert visitors into customers, and stand out in a crowded digital landscape.

Imagine: A local bakery doubling its orders because customers can place deliveries through a smooth online system. Or a startup getting funded faster because investors could clearly understand their product from a beautifully presented landing page. That’s the impact of a website done right.

Game Development

Games are more than play — they are storytelling, technology, and art combined. At ASARE, we build experiences that players remember, blending immersive design with engaging mechanics.

Our game development journey starts with concept ideation. We brainstorm characters, worlds, and gameplay loops that spark excitement. Prototypes are then built to test mechanics before full development begins. This ensures that fun is never an afterthought — it’s the foundation.

We focus on browser-based and indie-scale games, where creativity shines. Our team handles both the technical side (engines, frameworks, optimization) and the creative side (art, music, narrative). Every element is crafted to ensure players feel emotionally invested.

Our Game Development Process:

  1. Concept & Storyboarding
  2. Prototyping mechanics
  3. Visual design & artwork
  4. Coding & integration
  5. Testing & feedback loops
  6. Launch & post-release updates

Why our games stand out? Because we strike the balance between playability and originality. Many games look good but lack depth; many have depth but fail at polish. We aim for both.

Picture this: A casual arcade game that turns into a community favorite because of its addictive loop and leaderboard integration. Or a small story-driven prototype that becomes the seed for a funded indie studio. That’s the kind of ripple effect our work creates.

Whether it’s a simple arcade title or a narrative-driven prototype, ASARE ensures that gameplay is engaging, visuals are stunning, and performance is smooth across devices. Our goal is to turn your vision into a game that not only entertains but leaves a lasting impression.

Asset & Graphic Design

Digital projects come alive with the right visuals. Our asset and graphic design services cover everything from user interface elements to complex 3D models, animations, and branding kits. We understand that good design isn’t just about aesthetics — it’s about clarity, usability, and emotional impact.

Every project begins with an understanding of its identity: what it represents, who it serves, and what feeling it should evoke. From there, our designers create assets that seamlessly integrate into your application, website, or game.

What we deliver:

  • Custom icons and illustration sets
  • Complete UI/UX kits for apps and games
  • 2D and 3D models optimized for performance
  • Animations and motion graphics
  • Branding elements like logos and style guides
  • Marketing collateral (banners, posters, social media kits)

Our philosophy is simple: visuals should elevate the product, not overshadow it. That’s why we prioritize lightweight, reusable, and scalable assets that not only look professional but also work flawlessly within the project’s ecosystem.

Case in point: A mobile app gaining a 40% boost in user retention simply because the onboarding screens were clear, beautiful, and emotionally resonant. Or a game where polished icons and animations made the experience immersive and trustworthy. That’s how design silently shapes success.

With ASARE, your product doesn’t just function — it shines with visuals that resonate, engage, and inspire trust. Our assets aren’t just pixels; they’re your product’s personality, expressed visually.

Frequently Asked Questions

General

What services does ASARE specialize in?

We focus on three main areas: Web Development, Game Development, and Asset/Graphic Design. Each of these branches is backed by our philosophy of simplicity, creativity, and reliability.

Do you work with individuals as well as businesses?

Yes, we collaborate with solo creators, startups, and established businesses alike. Whether you need a personal portfolio or a full-fledged digital platform, we can help.

Can you handle urgent or short-deadline projects?

Depending on the scope, yes. For rush projects, we adjust our workflow and may dedicate additional resources to ensure timely delivery. Extra costs may apply.

Process & Collaboration

How do you start a new project?

Every project begins with a discovery phase: understanding your goals, audience, and unique requirements. From there, we prepare a proposal and timeline before moving into design and development.

Can I be involved during the development?

Absolutely. We encourage client involvement at every stage. You’ll have regular updates, demo previews, and checkpoints to provide feedback and request adjustments.

What communication tools do you use?

We use email, Zoom, Google Meet, Slack, and Trello/Asana for project management. This ensures transparent collaboration and smooth progress tracking.

What happens if I change my requirements midway?

Minor changes are usually accommodated, but larger scope shifts may affect the timeline and cost. We always discuss and confirm before proceeding.

Technical

Which technologies do you use for web development?

We work with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (React, Vue), backend frameworks like Node.js, and databases like MongoDB or MySQL. We choose based on project requirements.

Do you develop mobile apps as well?

While our core focus is web and games, we do build lightweight hybrid apps using frameworks like React Native when requested.

What platforms do you use for game development?

We primarily use Unity and Unreal Engine, along with WebGL for browser-based games. For art and assets, we use Blender, Photoshop, and Illustrator.

Are your designs optimized for performance?

Yes. All our websites and games are tested for speed, SEO readiness, and responsiveness. Assets are optimized to reduce load times without compromising quality.

Pricing & Payments

Do you offer fixed-price packages?

Yes. We provide fixed-price quotes after understanding your project scope. For long-term collaborations, hourly and retainer models are also available.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, PayPal, and other secure international payment gateways. For local clients, UPI and direct transfers are also supported.

Is there an upfront cost?

Yes, typically 30–40% of the project cost is paid upfront, with the remainder split into milestone-based payments.

Can I request a refund?

Refunds depend on the stage of the project. If work has already begun, partial refunds may be offered for unused portions. Full refunds are only possible if no work has started.

Support & Maintenance

Do you provide long-term maintenance?

Yes, we offer monthly or yearly maintenance plans. These include bug fixes, updates, backups, and security monitoring.

What if my website or game crashes?

In case of critical issues, we provide emergency support. Our goal is to restore functionality as quickly as possible.

Can you add new features after launch?

Of course. Many clients choose to expand their projects over time. We handle feature additions, redesigns, and scaling smoothly.

Will you train my team to use the product?

Yes. We provide documentation, guides, and optional training sessions so your team can manage the project independently.

Future & Scalability

Can my project scale as my business grows?

Absolutely. We design with scalability in mind — whether that’s adding new pages, expanding servers, or integrating advanced features later.

Do you work with advanced technologies like AI or AR/VR?

Yes. While our primary focus is web, games, and assets, we also explore AI-powered tools, AR/VR experiences, and other emerging technologies.

How do you future-proof websites and games?

We follow coding best practices, use modern frameworks, and regularly update libraries to ensure long-term stability and compatibility.

Do you help with marketing or SEO after development?

While we’re not a full-service marketing agency, we do provide SEO-friendly setups, performance optimization, and guidance on next steps to help your project gain visibility.
